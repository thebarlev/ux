A client rarely comes from a single click. They hear about you, check you out, compare, maybe come back a week later, and only then get in touch. Once you understand that journey, you can make sure something useful is waiting at every step. This guide follows it from start to finish, right up to the point where a client becomes someone who recommends you.

## Be there when they search

Most clients start with a search. And it's no longer only Google: more and more people ask ChatGPT or Perplexity things like "who's a good property lawyer near me". To show up there, you need a site with a clear page for each service, text that answers real questions, and consistent details about your business across the web.

## Win the comparison

Most clients look at a few options. Don't leave them guessing why they should choose you. Spell it out: who you're the best fit for, what's different about the way you work, and what the client gets in the end. An honest, specific difference works far better than "the best service around".

## Don't lose the people who almost got in touch

Lots of interested people disappear because of small friction: a long form, a phone number that's hard to find, a page that doesn't work well on a phone. Go through your own site on your phone as if you were a new client, and notice where you get stuck.

## Your next client comes from your current one

A happy client is the strongest marketing channel there is. Ask for a recommendation when the work wraps up and the experience is fresh, and give them an easy way to share, like a link to a specific service page on your site.
