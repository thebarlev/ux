When you run a small business, you're the manager, the salesperson and the whole marketing department. So when everyone tells you to be on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google and a newsletter, it feels like another full-time job. The good news: you don't need to be everywhere. You need to be good in one or two places, and build from there.

## The base: a website that works for you

Whatever channel you pick, clients eventually come to check you out. They land on your website, want to understand what you do, see that you're professional and find a quick way to get in touch. Social networks change their rules all the time, but your website is yours. It's where all that attention turns into an enquiry.

## Don't spread yourself thin

The usual mistake is opening five channels and abandoning all of them a month later. One channel you actually keep up is worth more. Once it works, add the next one.
