Someone looking for a lawyer, a clinic or an architect on their phone won't wait around. If your page doesn't open quickly, they tap back to the search results and pick the next name on the list. They won't email you to say your site is slow. They just leave. That's why speed isn't a technical detail. It's a question of how many enquiries you get.

## Why speed affects your bottom line

Google says openly that page experience, including how fast a page loads, is part of how it ranks results. So a slow site gets less exposure to begin with, and the people who do arrive leave sooner. Put those two together and you get fewer enquiries, even when your service is excellent.

## How to test it for free

Open Google's PageSpeed Insights, paste in your address and look at the mobile result. Don't panic about the score itself. Look at the first few recommendations, because they're usually the ones that matter most. It's also worth opening your site on your own phone over mobile data, not the office Wi-Fi, to see what a client really sees.

## Three things to fix this week

Start with images, since that's where most of the weight sits on a typical business site. Then go through your plugins and remove anything you haven't used in months. Finally, check that your homepage isn't loading a video or a huge slider before visitors even see your headline and your contact button.
