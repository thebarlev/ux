Paid ads can bring in clients quickly. But the moment you stop paying, the clients stop too. Plenty of businesses and professionals find they're working for the campaign instead of the campaign working for them. There's another way: build an organic system, one that keeps bringing in enquiries even in the months you don't spend a penny on ads.

## What "organic" actually means

An organic client is someone who found you without you buying their attention. They searched on Google and found your site, heard about you from a friend, or read something you wrote. It takes longer to build, but every step adds up.

## A page for every service

If you do several things, give each one its own page. An accountant who works with both freelancers and companies needs two pages, not one page trying to say everything. That way Google understands what each page is about, and visitors find what they came for straight away.

## Don't lose the people who already arrived

Lots of enquiries get lost at the very last step: the form is complicated, the phone number is hard to find, or nobody gets back to them. Make sure every page has one clear way to reach you, and that you reply quickly. It's the cheapest part of the whole system, and it makes the biggest difference.
