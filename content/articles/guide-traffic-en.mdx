A beautiful website that nobody visits won't bring you clients. At the same time, paying for every visit through ads gets expensive, and the traffic stops the moment the budget runs out. This guide is about building steady traffic, the kind that keeps coming even when you're not paying for it.

## 1. A page for every service

People search for a specific service, not just "lawyer". If each service has its own page with a clear title and a real explanation, Google and AI tools have far more reasons to show you. A single page that tries to cover everything usually ranks for nothing.

## 2. Answer the questions clients actually ask

Write down the ten questions clients ask you most, and answer them on your site in plain words. It helps with search, and especially with AI search, which looks for clear, direct answers.

## 3. Your Google Business Profile

If you serve a particular area, a Google Business Profile is probably the fastest way to get seen. Fill in every detail, add photos, link to your site, and ask happy clients to leave a review.

## 4. A fast, clear site

Traffic isn't worth much if visitors leave after two seconds. A site that loads quickly on mobile, with a headline that says what you do and an easy way to get in touch, turns more visits into enquiries.

## 5. Patience and measurement

Organic traffic builds slowly, and then it compounds. Once a month, check where visitors come from, which pages bring in enquiries, and what's worth strengthening.
