When a business decides it needs a website, the first question is almost always "WordPress, or something else?" There's no single right answer. It depends on how much time you have, who's going to look after the site, and how much you want to rely on other people every time something small needs changing.

## WordPress: flexible, but it needs looking after

WordPress is powerful and very flexible, and you can build almost anything with it. The price of that flexibility is maintenance. You pick hosting, install a theme and plugins, keep them updated, and take care of security and backups. Most business owners end up working with a developer or a studio, and waiting on them whenever they want to change something.

## Website builders: simple, but you do the work

Popular website builders take care of the technical side. Hosting is included and editing is drag and drop. On the other hand, you start from a template, and the design, the copy and making it fit your field are all on you. The result often looks like every other site built from the same template.

## An AI platform: the site is built for you

With this approach you tell it about your business, and the AI builds a complete site with pages, copy and images that suit your field. You make changes in a chat, the way you'd ask a person, or by clicking the text.

## So which one should you choose?

If you need a complex system with lots of custom development, WordPress is probably the way to go. If you run a practice, an office, a clinic or a studio and want a well-designed site that works without managing a project, it's worth looking at something that builds the site for you.
