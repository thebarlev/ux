Everyone wants to be on the first page of Google, ideally by tomorrow morning. The honest answer is that there's no magic button. But there are a few things you can improve this week, and sometimes they move your rankings faster than you'd expect. This guide covers them, along with what to stay away from.

## Start with what almost works

The fastest way up Google usually isn't building something new. It's improving a page that's already close. If a page of yours shows up on the second or third page of results, it has already shown Google it's relevant. A small fix to its title, description or content can push it forward. Google Search Console, Google's free tool, shows you exactly which searches your pages appear for, and in what position.

One thing to keep in mind: after you make a change, Google needs time to crawl the page again. Sometimes days, sometimes weeks. That's normal.

## A title that sounds like your client

People don't search for "advanced legal solutions". They search for "employment lawyer in Manchester". Write your titles in the words your clients actually type, and put the important part first. Give every page its own good title, not one title for the whole site.

## What not to do

Shortcuts look tempting: buying links, stuffing keywords, copying content from other sites. Google spots all of them, and the penalty can be a sharp drop that's very hard to climb back from. Real progress on Google is built on good pages that answer what people are looking for. It takes a little longer, but it lasts.
